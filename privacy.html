Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What Toro Studio accesses

When you connect TikTok, Toro Studio requests the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. It uses them only to show your display name and avatar, read your posting options (privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length) and upload and publish videos you choose.

What is stored

The TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, outside any cloud sync. Toro Studio has no server, database or analytics, and it does not collect, sell or share personal data with third parties.

Videos

Videos are read from the creator's local folders and sent directly to TikTok's API. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Toro Studio at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes access.
