TORO STUDIO

Toro Studio is a publishing tool built for the creator José Toro (TikTok @jtoros_, Kick yosoytoro). It turns highlights from his live streams into short vertical clips and lets him post them to his own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator connects his TikTok account with TikTok Login (OAuth).
  2. He picks a finished clip, reviews the preview and edits the title.
  3. He chooses who can view the video, whether to allow comments, duets and stitches, and discloses any commercial content.
  4. He confirms TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and posts. The tool shows the publishing status until TikTok finishes processing.

Toro Studio only posts content the creator owns, only to the account he connected, and only after he presses Post to TikTok.
